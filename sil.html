MATH 429, INTRODUCTION TO TOPOLOGY
Metric spaces, topological spaces, separation axioms, product spaces, identification spaces, compactness, connectedness, fundamental group.

  • Instructor: Anton Petrunin
  • Class: MoWeFr 10:10--11:00, Business Bldg 102
  • Book: "A First Course in Algebraic Topology" by C. Kosniowski
  • Additional materials:
    • [H] Notes on Introductory Point-Set Topology by A. Hatcher.
    • [VIKN] Elementary Topology: Problem Textbook by O. Viro, O. Ivanov, V. Kharlamov, N. Netsvetaev.
  • Office hours: McAllister 333 We 11:10--12:00, or by appointment at psu.zoom.us/my/anton.petrunin.
